Project Overview

This case study covers a real-world style production incident where a downstream dependency caused intermittent failures in the payment service. It demonstrates how I lead triage, communicate clearly, and drive long-term improvements.

Incident Timeline

  • 09:02 — Monitoring alerts triggered for elevated error rate in payment endpoint.
  • 09:05 — Acknowledged alert, joined incident bridge, and began initial triage.
  • 09:12 — Confirmed partial degradation using Splunk queries and Dynatrace service maps.
  • 09:20 — Isolated root cause through trace analysis and downstream metrics.
  • 09:35 — Applied mitigation (circuit breaker + traffic rerouting).
  • 10:10 — Full recovery confirmed and post-incident review initiated.

Root Cause

High latency in a downstream address verification service caused timeouts in the main payment flow. The dependency was not sufficiently isolated, allowing the issue to impact overall transaction success rate.

Resolution & Improvements

  • Activated circuit breaker and fallback logic to prevent total outage.
  • Improved monitoring with better alerting on downstream latency and transaction success rates.
  • Collaborated with engineering to implement retries with backoff and graceful degradation.
  • Updated runbooks, monitoring thresholds, and added synthetic checks for the critical dependency.

Support Engineer Insight

Partial degradations can often be more damaging than full outages because they are harder to detect quickly. Strong observability, calm leadership during incidents, and a focus on long-term resilience are essential when supporting critical financial infrastructure.

This experience directly translates to supporting platforms like Circle, where reliability and clear communication during incidents are paramount.
