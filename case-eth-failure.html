Investigation

A user reported that their Uniswap swap transaction failed and gas was consumed, but no tokens were received.

Steps Taken

  • Obtained the transaction hash and opened it on Etherscan.
  • Confirmed the transaction status was "Failed" and reviewed the revert reason.
  • Decoded the input data to examine swap parameters (amountIn, amountOutMinimum, path, deadline).
  • Inspected internal transactions and event logs to understand contract interactions.
  • Checked the liquidity pool state and price movement at the exact block the transaction was mined.
  • Compared the quoted price vs actual execution conditions.

Root Cause

The transaction reverted because the price moved beyond the user’s slippage tolerance between the time the quote was received and when the transaction was executed. This is a common occurrence during volatile market conditions or when trading lower-liquidity tokens.

Resolution & Outcome

  • Explained slippage mechanics to the user in clear, non-technical language.
  • Recommended appropriate slippage tolerance settings based on token volatility and trade size.
  • Advised retrying with a higher slippage tolerance or splitting the trade into smaller amounts.
  • Documented the case with Etherscan screenshots, decoded parameters, and key learnings.
  • Added this scenario to my personal DeFi troubleshooting guide.

Support Engineer Insight

Even seemingly simple user-reported “failed transaction” cases require methodical on-chain analysis. Being able to quickly determine whether the issue is user error, market conditions, or a protocol bug is a highly valuable skill when supporting crypto-native platforms like Circle.

This type of investigation builds confidence in explaining blockchain behaviour to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
